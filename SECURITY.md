# Security Policies and Procedures

This document outlines security procedures and general policies for the
`<cml-mcp>` project.

- [Reporting a Bug](#reporting-a-bug)
- [Disclosure Policy](#disclosure-policy)
- [Comments on this Policy](#comments-on-this-policy)

## Reporting a Bug

The `<cml-mcp>` team and community take all security bugs in
`<cml-mcp>` seriously. Thank you for improving the security of
`<cml-mcp>`. We appreciate your efforts and responsible disclosure and
will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

Report security bugs by emailing `jclarke@marcuscom.com`.

The lead maintainer will acknowledge your email as soon as possible.
Typically, this will be within 48 hours, but circumstances may require
it take longer. After the initial reply to your report, the author will
attempt to resolve the issue and notify you of the remediation.

## Disclosure Policy

When the maintainer receives a security bug report, they will coordinate the fix and release process,
involving the following steps:

- Confirm the problem and determine the affected versions.
- Audit code to find any potential similar problems.
- Prepare fixes for all releases still under maintenance. These fixes will be
  released as quickly as possible.

## Comments on this Policy

If you have suggestions on how this process could be improved please submit a
pull request.
